# Sprint 1 Plan

## Project Name
Student Management Portal

## Sprint Duration
1 week

## Sprint Goal
To prepare the initial design and structure of the Student Management Portal.

## Sprint Backlog
- Create the initial project structure.
- Design a student registration form.
- Design a student records page.
- Prepare project documentation.
- Review the completed work.

## Tasks and Responsibilities
- Team members will prepare the initial project files.
- The team will design the student registration form.
- The team will create a basic student records page.
- The team will review the design and documentation.

## Expected Deliverables
- Initial project structure.
- Student registration form design.
- Student records page design.
- Updated project documentation.

## Sprint Review
The team will demonstrate the completed work and verify whether the Sprint Goal has been achieved.

## Sprint Retrospective
The team will discuss what went well, identify problems, and suggest improvements for the next Sprint.
